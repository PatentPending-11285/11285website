Proudly Sponsored By Surface Optics Corporation

We are proud to partner with Surface Optics Corporation, a San Diego-based company specializing in optical engineering, advanced imaging technology, and precision measurement solutions.

Surface Optics develops specialized scientific instruments, optical measurement systems, hyperspectral imaging technology, and advanced surface coatings. Its technologies support a range of industries and applications, including aerospace, space exploration, scientific research, and manufacturing.

Their commitment to engineering excellence, scientific innovation, and solving complex technical challenges reflects many of the same values we strive to uphold as a FIRST Tech Challenge robotics team.

We sincerely thank Surface Optics Corporation for supporting Patent Pending 11285. Their partnership helps us continue designing, building, programming, and competing while pursuing our passion for STEM and robotics.
