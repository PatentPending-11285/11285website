Into The Deep V1

Designed by Sohm Shah (2024)

Initial proof-of-concept chassis configured for baseline game piece pickup and autonomous deadwheel routing.

  • Drivetrain: goBILDA Strafer Chassis (Mecanum)
  • Intake: Active TPU compliant wheel roller
  • Vision: OpenCV target tracking

Into The Deep V2

Designed by Sohm Shah (2024)

Chassis revision integrating custom mounting brackets and initial multi-stage linear slide extension geometry.

  • Structure: Custom laser-cut aluminum plates
  • Lift: Multi-stage custom linear slides
  • Focus: High basket reaching stability

Into The Deep V2.5

Designed by Sohm Shah (2024–2025)

Mid-season optimization focusing on mass reduction, wire routing management, and intake compliance.

  • Weight: Strategic pocketing for structural efficiency
  • Sensors: High-frequency optical distance sensors
  • Focus: Reliability and autonomous path repeatability

Into The Deep V3

Designed by Sohm Shah (2025)

League tournament build featuring CNC polycarbonate plates, reduced overall weight, and high-speed linear slides.

  • Chassis: Low-profile Mecanum drive
  • Control: RoadRunner spline trajectory profiling
  • Focus: Maximum cycle speed & alliance synergy

Into The Deep V4

Designed by Sohm Shah (2025)

Final championship revision with active dual-servo pitch & roll wrist claw end-effector and 3-wheel pinpoint deadwheel odometry.

  • End-Effector: Precision pitch & roll wrist claw
  • Odometry: 3-wheel pinpoint tracking module
  • Weight: Optimized structure down to 28 lbs
